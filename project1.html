The context

Project Overview

The UX Design Club is a student organization at UT Austin focused on connecting students interested in user experience design through educational workshops, industry speaker events, and collaborative activities.

I later served as Vice-President, taking on additional responsibilities in event promotion, coordination, and logistics.

Marketing Officer · Aug 2023 – Jul 2025

Planning and executing event promotion

As Marketing Officer, I helped plan and execute promotional campaigns for approximately 3–4 events per semester. My responsibilities included designing social media content, writing promotional copy, scheduling posts, publicizing events across campus, and encouraging students to register and participate.

The Challenge

Our goal was to increase awareness of the club, attract students to events, and encourage sustained participation in the UX design community.

This required understanding what students wanted from the organization, communicating the value of upcoming events, and reaching audiences through multiple channels.

My Approach

The RACE Marketing Framework

The following case study organizes my work retrospectively using the established RACE framework: Plan, Reach, Act, Convert, Engage.

01PLAN

Understanding Our Audience

To better understand member interests, I created and distributed Google Forms surveys, typically at the beginning of a semester.

  • Students could indicate which activities interested them most, including speaker panels, portfolio workshops, skills workshops, industry discussions, and social events. They could also suggest their own ideas.
  • I reviewed the responses and participated in planning events based on member preferences.

02REACH

Building Awareness

I helped promote club events through Instagram, LinkedIn, university newsletters, classroom announcements, and in-person outreach.

  • For each campaign, I designed promotional graphics using Canva and Adobe Express, wrote captions, and scheduled posts using Buffer.
  • We also collaborated with other student organizations, including a computer science-focused organization for a hackathon, to connect with students who shared overlapping interests.

03ACT

Generating Interest

Our promotional content highlighted what students could gain from attending an event.

  • For industry speaker events, I created posts communicating the speakers' professional backgrounds, the topics being discussed, and essential event details.
  • I also observed that posts featuring authentic photographs—such as previous club events and group photos—generally attracted more views and likes than posts consisting entirely of graphics and text.
  • This insight helped inform subsequent content decisions.

04CONVERT

Encouraging Event Registration

To turn interest into participation, I incorporated clear calls to action into promotional materials.

  • Students could access event registration through Linktree, RSVP links, and QR codes included in event graphics.
  • I helped promote these registration opportunities across our communication channels and through in-person outreach.

05ENGAGE

Sustaining Community Participation

Our marketing continued beyond the event itself.

  • I created LinkedIn event recaps featuring photographs, speaker insights, and guest tags. These posts highlighted the value of attending club events and encouraged students to participate in future activities.
  • We maintained a recurring event schedule, generally organizing approximately one event per month, to provide ongoing opportunities for members to connect and learn.

Vice-President

Event coordination & operations

Key Takeaways

This experience taught me that effective marketing involves more than producing attractive graphics.

By combining audience feedback, relevant messaging, multichannel promotion, clear calls to action, and performance observations, I learned how marketing activities can support measurable community growth.

It also helped me develop a more strategic approach to content creation: understanding the audience, communicating value, and improving future campaigns based on what works.
